A thick lawn is the best weed control there is. Weeds move into thin, bare spots, and overseeding (spreading new seed over an existing lawn) fills those spots with grass first.

## When to overseed

The best time in Guelph is **late August to late September**. The soil is warm, nights are cool, and the seed has several weeks to establish before winter. Spring works too, but new seedlings then have to fight weeds and summer heat.

## How to do it

1. **Mow short.** Cut the lawn lower than usual and bag the clippings so seed can reach the soil.
2. **Rake or dethatch.** Loosen the surface and pull out dead grass so seed makes contact with the soil.
3. **Top dress bare patches.** A thin layer of soil or triple mix over bare spots gives seed a good bed.
4. **Spread seed evenly.** Use a blend suited to your yard, like a shade mix under trees or a sun mix in open areas.
5. **Feed it.** A starter fertilizer helps young roots develop.
6. **Water lightly and often.** Keep the top layer moist until the seed sprouts, usually 7 to 21 days depending on the grass type.

## Fertilizing through the year

- **Spring:** a light feed once the grass is actively growing.
- **Early fall:** the most important feed of the year. It builds roots and helps the lawn recover from summer.
- **Skip midsummer** feeding during heat and drought, when grass is stressed.

## Mowing new grass

Wait until the new grass is 8 to 10 cm tall before mowing, and keep the mower on a high setting for the rest of the season.
